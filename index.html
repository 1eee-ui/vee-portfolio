FAQ

Questions?
Answers.

Can't find yours? Just ask me.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

Not always. Simple sites run well on free hosting. Sites with a server or database may need a small paid plan — I tell you before we start. If you want your own domain, you buy it in your name and I connect it.

Can you build from my Figma design?

Yes. I can build from your design, or create one based on websites you like.

Who owns the website after delivery?

You do. All code and accounts are created in your name, and I hand everything over in writing at the end.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, texts and photos (or I prepare placeholder content), and two or three websites you like.

Can you build a mobile app too?

Yes — an installable web app, or an Android / iOS app from the same code. I prepare the store builds and listings and guide you through publishing. The store accounts are yours (Google Play $25 once, Apple $99 per year), and approval is decided by Google and Apple.
