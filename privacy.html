Privacy

Short version

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it has no forms. I only receive your data if you write to me.

Who is responsible

What happens when you visit

When you contact me

Your rights

You can ask me to show, correct or delete any data I have about you — just send an e-mail. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
